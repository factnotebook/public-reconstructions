# FactNotebook Public Reconstruction Registry

Public, inspectable **outputs** from bounded FactNotebook reconstructions.
Publication of a reconstruction does **not** imply publication of the reconstruction engine. Each artifact declares its observation surface, proposition, result, provenance, limitations and custody information — what FactNotebook asserts and on what, not how it computes.

This registry is **not open source**: the reconstruction engine, models and reading rules are not published. What is published is the sealed *output* of each reconstruction and the exact manifest committed to by its Reconstruction ID. (This is internal determinism — the manifest fixes what produced the result — not independent external reproducibility: the engine is not public, so a third party cannot re-run the reconstruction itself.)
